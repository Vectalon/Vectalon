Privacy Policy

Effective 5 September 2026

Vectalon is an independent software product operated from India. This policy explains the limited information processed by the Vectalon website, licensing service, and support systems.

Information we process

We process account identifiers supplied by GitHub for trial eligibility; email, order, subscription, tax, and payment-status metadata supplied by Lemon Squeezy; license, activation, revocation, and product-usage counters; and information you deliberately send through waitlist or support forms. Lemon Squeezy processes payment-card details as merchant of record; Vectalon does not receive or store full card details.

Your source code

Vectalon’s developer harness runs locally. We do not upload or store your source code through ordinary product use. Optional support bundles or telemetry are transmitted only when you choose to enable or submit them and are designed to redact common secrets.

Purpose and processors

We use this information to provide trials, issue and validate licenses, deliver transactional email, prevent abuse, operate support, and maintain service security. Relevant processors include GitHub, Lemon Squeezy, Vercel, Supabase, and Resend, which may process data in other countries under their own privacy terms.

Retention and rights

Order and licensing records are retained as needed for fulfillment, fraud prevention, accounting, legal obligations, and dispute handling. Support and waitlist data are removed when no longer needed. To request access, correction, or deletion where applicable, email privacy@vectalon.in. Some transaction records must be retained by law or by the merchant of record.

Security and changes

We use access controls, signed credentials, encrypted transport, and restricted production storage. No system is perfectly secure. Material policy changes will be posted here with a new effective date.

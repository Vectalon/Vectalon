Health Data Disclaimer

Version 1.0.0

Effective date: August 31, 2026  |  Vectalon

IMPORTANT: Family OS is not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. The health record features are for personal organization only and should never be used as a substitute for professional medical care.

1. Not a Medical Device

Family OS is a family organization application. It is not:

  • A medical device or health device
  • FDA-cleared or CE-marked for medical use
  • A HIPAA-covered entity or business associate
  • A clinical decision support system
  • A telemedicine or remote monitoring platform
  • A pharmacy, prescription, or medication management tool

2. What Family OS Does

The health record feature in Family OS allows you to:

  • Organize personal health-related notes and information
  • Store dates and categories of health-related events you enter
  • Keep records organized for your own reference
  • Share health information with family members you choose

All health data in Family OS is user-entered. The app does not generate, interpret, or analyze health data. It does not connect to medical devices, labs, pharmacies, or healthcare providers.

3. What Family OS Does NOT Do

Family OS does not:
  • Provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations
  • Interpret lab results, test values, or medical records
  • Suggest medications, dosages, or treatment changes
  • Monitor health conditions or predict health outcomes
  • Replace consultation with a qualified healthcare provider
  • Provide emergency medical information or triage
  • Verify the accuracy of health information you enter
  • Provide clinical-grade data storage or backup

4. Your Responsibilities

  • Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical decisions
  • Never use Family OS as a substitute for professional medical advice
  • Never delay seeking medical care because of information in the app
  • Verify all health information with your healthcare provider
  • Keep your own medical records — Family OS is a convenience tool, not a primary record system
  • In an emergency, call your local emergency number immediately — do not use the app

5. Data Accuracy

Family OS does not verify the accuracy of health information you enter. You are solely responsible for:

  • The accuracy of all health data you enter
  • Keeping your health information up to date
  • Ensuring your health data is not misleading or incomplete
  • Understanding the limitations of your own health data

6. No Clinical Use

Family OS is not intended for clinical use by healthcare providers. It should not be used to:

  • Record clinical observations or diagnoses
  • Track patient outcomes or treatment efficacy
  • Store protected health information (PHI) subject to HIPAA
  • Meet any regulatory or compliance requirements for health data
  • Replace Electronic Health Record (EHR) or Electronic Medical Record (EMR) systems

7. Emergency Use

In a medical emergency, always call your local emergency number (911, 112, 999, etc.) or go to the nearest emergency room. Never use Family OS for emergency medical information or decisions.

8. Third-Party Health Information

Any health information displayed in Family OS comes from you or sources you choose. Family OS does not independently verify health information from third parties. You are responsible for evaluating the accuracy and appropriateness of any health information you store or share.

9. No Insurance or Billing

Family OS does not interact with health insurance providers, process medical billing, or generate insurance claims. Health records stored in the app are personal records only.

  • Decisions made based on health information stored in the app
  • Inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated health data you enter
  • Reliance on Family OS as a substitute for professional medical advice
  • Any health outcome related to your use of the app

11. Regulatory Status

Family OS has not been evaluated or approved by the FDA (U.S. Food and Drug Administration), CE (European Conformity), TGA (Australia), or any other medical device regulatory body. It is not classified as a medical device in any jurisdiction.

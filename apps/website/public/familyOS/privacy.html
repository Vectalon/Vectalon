This policy applies globally. Where local law provides stronger protections (e.g., GDPR in the EEA, LGPD in Brazil, PIPA in South Korea), those protections apply in addition to this policy.

5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are in the EEA or UK, we process your data under these legal bases:

  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)): Data needed to provide the core service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): Error reporting and anonymous analytics to maintain and improve the product. We balance this against your privacy rights and minimize data collection.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): Where required by local law, we obtain your consent before processing certain data (e.g., analytics in the EEA, push notifications).

6. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the Family OS service
  • To send task reminders and notifications you have configured
  • To sync your household data across your devices
  • To store and serve your uploaded documents securely
  • To detect, diagnose, and fix errors (crash reports)
  • To improve the product through anonymous usage analytics
  • To communicate with you about your account or service changes

We never use your data for targeted advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making.

8. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored with:

  • TLS 1.3 encryption in transit for all API communication
  • AES-256 encryption at rest for stored data and files
  • Row-level security (RLS) ensuring you can only access your own household's data
  • Signed, time-limited URLs for document access (expire after 1 hour)
  • Automatic session management with token refresh and secure storage
  • Isolated households — data from different households is completely segregated at the database level

No system is 100% secure. We implement industry-standard practices and continuously monitor for threats.

Depending on your Supabase project configuration, your data may be stored in the United States or Europe. When data is transferred across borders, we rely on:

11. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you have the following rights:

EEA and UK residents (GDPR)

  • Access — Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure — Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction — Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances
  • Portability — Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection — Object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw consent — Where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time
  • Lodge a complaint — File a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority

All users

  • Export: Download all your household data from Settings → Privacy → Export Data
  • Delete: Delete your account and all data from Settings → Privacy → Delete Account
  • Notifications: Control which notifications you receive in Settings → Notifications
  • Analytics: Opt out of anonymous analytics in Settings → Privacy

To exercise any right, email privacy@vectalon.in. We will respond within 30 days.

12. California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) give you additional rights:

  • Right to know — What personal information we collect, use, and disclose
  • Right to delete — Request deletion of personal information
  • Right to opt out of sale — We do not sell personal information. This right does not apply.
  • Right to non-discrimination — We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights
  • Right to correct — Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information — We do not use sensitive personal information beyond what is necessary for the service

To exercise these rights, email privacy@vectalon.in with "CCPA Request" in the subject line.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

13. Children's Privacy

Family OS is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not create accounts for children under 18.

You may add family members who are minors as managed profiles. All data about minors is entered and managed by a parent or guardian. We do not collect data directly from children and do not knowingly process children's personal data.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, please contact us immediately at privacy@vectalon.in and we will delete it promptly.

14. Cookies and Tracking

Family OS is a native mobile application and does not use browser cookies.

We use the following for product improvement (not advertising):

  • PostHog: Anonymous product analytics (screen views, feature usage). No personal identifiers are sent. You can opt out in Settings.
  • Sentry: Error and crash reporting. PII is scrubbed before submission.

We do not use advertising SDKs, fingerprinting, or cross-app tracking.
